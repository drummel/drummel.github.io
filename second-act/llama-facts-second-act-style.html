Six wild facts that prove llamas are nature's most underrated power-up.

Spit Snipers

Llamas can spit up to 15 feet with terrifying accuracy. They primarily do this to settle disputes with other llamas — but will absolutely draft you into the conflict if you're standing too close.

Learn More

Guard Llamas

Ranchers deploy single llamas to guard sheep and goats against coyotes and foxes. The llama bonds with the flock, then absolutely bodies any predator stupid enough to approach. One llama. Whole flock. Protected.

Learn More

Altitude Kings

Llamas thrive at elevations above 13,000 feet in the Andes, where most animals would collapse. Their blood has uniquely shaped red cells that capture oxygen more efficiently than any other mammal.

Learn More

Therapy Mode

Llamas are certified therapy animals across North America. Their calm demeanor and soft fleece make them naturals at reducing anxiety in hospitals and nursing homes. The vibe? Impeccable.

Learn More

Eco Warriors

Unlike hooved animals, llamas have soft padded feet that don't tear up terrain. They're the only pack animals certified for use in U.S. wilderness areas because they leave virtually zero environmental footprint.

Learn More

200+ Flavors

Llama fleece comes in over 200 natural color variations — more than any other fiber-producing animal on the planet. No dye required. Nature really said "here's the full Pantone library."
