the challenge

where things stand

  • Limited SEO taxonomy
  • No unified reporting

offer discovery &
business profiles

consumer journey

  • Search or browse by category + geography
  • Category + geo landing pages surface relevant offers
  • Offers gated behind RSVP — creates engagement signal
  • Business profiles with gallery, CTAs, optional IG embed
  • Actions: website click, call, directions, share, save, contact

"request a deal" signal

Consumers flag businesses they want offers from — even non-advertisers. Businesses can claim profiles and suggest edits.

Result: Every request becomes a franchisee sales tool — real consumer demand data delivered to the sales pipeline.

offer archive &
pdf batch processing

ingestion pipeline

  • PDFs received from print vendor
  • Automated categorization: market, category, advertiser, date
  • Admin correction and mapping tools
  • Franchisee access scoped by territory

historical migration

  • Migrate existing offer database from current platform
  • Archive browsable by market / date / category / advertiser
  • Download and export for sales presentations
  • Full data continuity — nothing gets left behind

Result: Franchisees get instant access to their full offer history — a sales asset, not a filing cabinet.

lead routing

Current limitation: Leads route based on the page they originated from — not where the prospect is actually located.

current state

  • Page-based routing via microsites
  • No geographic intelligence
  • Leads fall through the cracks when geography doesn't match page ownership

proposed solution

  • True geographic routing: city, ZIP, territory rules
  • Override capability for edge cases
  • Full audit trail on every routing decision
  • CRM sync ensures no lead is lost or misrouted
