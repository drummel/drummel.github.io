🎒 What to Pack

Everything you'll need for hiking, beach time, stargazing, and cozy cabin vibes.

🥾

Hiking Essentials

  • 1 Hiking shoes/boots (trails can be muddy)
  • 2 Pairs hiking socks
  • 1 Daypack/backpack
  • 2 Reusable water bottles
  • 1 Pair trekking poles (optional, helpful for Ewoldsen)
  • 1 Trail map/offline map downloaded
🧥

Layers & Weather Gear

  • 1 Rain jacket (light, packable)
  • 1 Warm fleece or puffer jacket
  • 2 Long sleeve base layers
  • 2 Comfy pants/leggings for hiking
  • 1 Beanie or warm hat
  • 1 Pair gloves (for stargazing nights)
  • 1 Scarf or neck gaiter
🧺

Beach & Picnic Gear

  • 1 Picnic blanket (waterproof backing ideal)
  • 1 Small cooler bag
  • 1 Set reusable utensils
  • 1 Corkscrew/wine opener
  • 2 Beach towels
  • 1 Windbreaker (beaches are breezy)
  • 1 Pair waterproof shoes (for Calla Lily Valley!)
🌌

Stargazing & Evening

  • 1 Warm blanket (cabin to beach transport)
  • 1 Thermos for hot cocoa/coffee
  • 1 Red-light headlamp (preserves night vision)
  • 1 Binoculars (doubles for whale watching!)
  • 1 Star chart app downloaded
  • 2 Cozy cabin outfits
📸

Camera & Tech

  • 1 Camera + charger
  • 1 Phone tripod or GorillaPod
  • 1 Portable battery pack
  • 1 Car charger
  • 1 Offline maps downloaded (spotty service!)
  • 1 Good playlist downloaded
✨

Don't Forget

  • 1 Sunglasses
  • 1 Sunscreen (winter sun is real)
  • 1 Lip balm with SPF
  • 1 First aid kit (small)
  • 1 Hand sanitizer
  • 1 Book or cards for cabin time
  • $ Cash (some places don't take cards)

🥗 Picnic Snack Ideas

Easy-prep ideas for beach picnics and trail fuel. Light on dairy, big on flavor!

🍳

Make Ahead (Night Before)

  • Deviled eggs
  • Pasta salad with olive oil dressing
  • Marinated olives & artichoke hearts
  • Hard boiled eggs (pre-peeled)
  • Homemade trail mix
  • Bruschetta topping (add to bread on-site)
  • Caprese skewers (use dairy-free mozz option)
  • Pickled veggies
🛒

Grab & Go (Store-Bought)

  • Hummus + pita chips
  • Salami or prosciutto
  • Crackers & breadsticks
  • Castelvetrano olives
  • Roasted nuts (marcona almonds!)
  • Dark chocolate
  • Dried mango or apricots
  • Fancy pickles (cornichons)
  • Dairy-free cheese wedges
🍇

Fresh & Simple

  • Grapes (seedless, easy to share)
  • Apple slices + almond butter
  • Clementines or mandarins
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Snap peas & carrots
  • Berries in a container
  • Avocado (bring salt & lime!)
  • Cucumber slices
🥪

Heartier Trail Fuel

  • Wraps (make morning-of, hold up well)
  • Peanut butter & banana sandwiches
  • Chicken salad cups (dairy-free mayo)
  • Rice balls / onigiri
  • Energy balls (dates + nuts + coconut)
  • Veggie spring rolls
  • Pinwheel sandwiches
  • Empanadas (buy from bakery)
🥤

Drinks & Treats

  • Sparkling water (cans travel well)
  • Wine (screw-top for easy beach access)
  • Hot cocoa in thermos
  • Cold brew coffee
  • Ginger beer or kombucha
  • Fancy lemonade
  • Hot cider for stargazing nights
💡

Pro Tips

  • Bruno's in Carmel makes great picnic sandwiches
  • Big Sur Deli is perfect for trail-ready grub
  • Pack a small cutting board for beach spreads
  • Bring napkins - always need more than you think
  • Reusable containers > plastic bags
  • Freeze water bottles = ice packs that become drinks
  • Lactaid pills are lifesavers for cheese moments
